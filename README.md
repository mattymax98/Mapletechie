# Mapletechie

**Independent Canadian technology publication** · [mapletechie.com](https://www.mapletechie.com)

Mapletechie covers artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, consumer technology,
electric vehicles, and digital innovation. Our reporting and practical guides
focus on what technology means for readers, with a particular emphasis on
Canadian context.

## What we're building

- A reader-friendly publication for technology news, analysis, and explainers
- Topic and category pages that make useful coverage easier to discover
- Editorial tools for managing articles and reviewing updates
- Newsletter, reader engagement, and site analytics features

## Technology

Mapletechie is a TypeScript monorepo built with:

- **Frontend:** React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, and shadcn/ui
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express, and Drizzle ORM
- **Database:** PostgreSQL
- **Workspace:** pnpm
